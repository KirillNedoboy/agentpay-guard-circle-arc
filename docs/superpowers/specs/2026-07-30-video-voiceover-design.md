# AgentPay Guard Demo Voiceover Design

**Date:** 2026-07-30  
**Status:** Approved and implemented

## Goal

Improve the English narration of the existing 16:9 AgentPay Guard hackathon demo without changing the visual evidence, product UI, or safety claims.

## Approach

- Keep the existing 1280×720 H.264 video scenes and overlays.
- Replace the existing audio track with a full neural narration generated by Edge TTS.
- Use `en-US-AriaNeural` at a slightly accelerated rate so the narration remains clear while fitting the judge-facing cut.
- Extend only the final static safety card by approximately five seconds so the narration is not cut off.
- Encode a universal MP4 with H.264 video, AAC mono audio, and yuv420p pixel format.

## Narrative

The narration explains:

1. AgentPay Guard as a payment firewall for autonomous agents.
2. Deterministic validation of destination, amount, asset, chain, and evidence.
3. The `ALLOW` / `REVIEW` / `BLOCK` decision model with risk score and audit receipt.
4. The mock rail preview and append-only audit trail.
5. The explicit boundary: no wallet, private keys, signing, live settlement, or real funds.
6. Why the boundary makes autonomous payments observable before execution.

## Verification criteria

- Video remains 1280×720 and visually intact.
- Title card remains readable.
- Main product demo and subtitles remain readable.
- Final safety card remains visible and coherent.
- Video and audio streams have matching duration.
- Audio is present throughout the narration and does not end before the final card.
- The MP4 is publicly retrievable from the canonical GitHub raw URL.
